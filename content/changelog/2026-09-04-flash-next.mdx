Reader now answers you with **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next**, an open-weight Qwen4-architecture preview: a 125B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with about 6B parameters active per token, NVFP4-quantized, served by **vLLM** and fronted by **LiteLLM**.

Several models have come and gone since the April move to local inference. The swaps never touched agent code: Reader talks to a stable LiteLLM alias, so the model behind it can change without the site noticing.

The public site gets its own LiteLLM alias with its own prefix-cache salt, so its requests can't be timed against the private tools sharing the same engine.
